Privacy Policy

Nico's Science Fun · Effective October 16, 2026

We do not collect any data. Nico's Science Fun does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information or usage data from anyone, including children.

  • No accounts, sign-ins, names, emails, or photos.
  • No advertising and no ad networks.
  • No analytics, tracking, or third-party SDKs.
  • No in-app purchases and no links out of the app.
  • The app makes no network requests while you play.

Game progress (stars earned and sound settings) is saved only on your device using local storage. It never leaves the device, and deleting the app removes it.

The optional voice feature uses your device's built-in text-to-speech. No audio is recorded.

Because we collect no information, the app is designed to meet the requirements of COPPA, GDPR-K and Apple's Kids Category.

If this policy ever changes, we will update this page and the date above.
