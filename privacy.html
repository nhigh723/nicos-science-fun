Privacy Policy

Fizby's Fun & Learn · Effective October 27, 2026

We do not collect any data. Fizby's Fun & Learn does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information or usage data from anyone, including children.

  • No accounts, sign-ins, emails, or photos.
  • No advertising and no ad networks.
  • No analytics, tracking, or third-party SDKs.
  • The microphone and camera are never used.

On your device only

Game progress, stickers, settings and any kid profile names you type in are saved only on your device using local storage. They never leave the device, and deleting the app removes them. Fizby's voice is pre-recorded audio included in the app.

In-app purchases

The first 2 levels of every game are free. A grown-up can unlock everything with Fizby Club (an auto-renewing subscription: $4.99 per month or $29.99 per year) or Fizby Forever ($59.99, a one-time purchase). Prices are in US dollars and may vary by country.

All purchases, restores and subscription management are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. Payment and account details go to Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. We never receive or store your payment information, Apple ID, or any personal data. The app asks Apple only whether a purchase is active, so it can unlock the games on your device.

Links out of the app

There are no ads and no links for children. The only links out of the app (Terms of Use, this Privacy Policy, and Manage Subscription) are in the grown-up area, behind a parental gate that asks a grown-up to hold a button and answer a math question.

Because we collect no information, the app is designed to meet the requirements of COPPA, GDPR-K and Apple's Kids Category.

If this policy ever changes, we will update this page and the date above.
