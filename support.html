Fizby's Fun & Learn Support

Tap a big picture on the home screen to pick a world, then tap a game. Fizby says every goal out loud, and a hint hand shows what to do.

Progress: saved on your device. Sound, music, voice: tap the gear on the title screen (grown-up area).

Purchases

The first 2 levels of every game are free. To unlock everything, a grown-up taps the gear on the title screen (or the gold "Unlock all levels" button), passes the grown-up check, and picks Fizby Club ($4.99/month or $29.99/year, renews automatically) or Fizby Forever ($59.99 one-time). Payment is charged to your Apple ID. Family Sharing is included.

Restore purchases

New phone or iPad, or reinstalled the app? Open the grown-up area (gear, then the grown-up check), tap Fizby Club, then tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple ID you bought with.

Cancel a subscription

Open Settings > [your name] (Apple ID) > Subscriptions, choose Fizby Club, then tap Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before it renews to avoid the next charge. You can also tap Manage Subscription in the app's Fizby Club screen. For refunds, visit reportaproblem.apple.com.
